Plan Your Monthly Budget in 5 Simple Steps

November 18, 2026 · By BrightGuide Daily Team

Many people earn a fair income but still feel broke at the end of the month. The problem is usually not income, it is the absence of a plan. A simple monthly budget solves this, and you do not need any special software to start.

Step 1: Write Down Your Real Income

Note the exact amount that reaches your account each month after deductions. If your income changes from month to month, use the lowest recent month as your base.

Step 2: List Every Expense

Write down rent, bills, groceries, transport, mobile recharge and small daily spending. Small expenses are where most money quietly disappears.

Step 3: Divide Money Into Three Parts

A popular method is the 50-30-20 rule. About 50 percent goes to needs, 30 percent to wants, and 20 percent to savings or extra debt payment. Adjust the percentages to your city and family size.

Step 4: Pay Yourself First

Move the savings part to a separate account on the day income arrives, not at the end of the month. What is left is what you can safely spend.

Step 5: Review Every Week

Ten minutes a week is enough. Compare your plan with your actual spending and fix one small leak at a time. Budgets fail when people try to change everything in one day.

Start this month, even if the plan is imperfect. A rough budget followed honestly beats a perfect budget that exists only on paper.

Social Media Basics for Small Businesses: Start Simple, Stay Consistent

October 8, 2026 · By BrightGuide Daily Team

SM

Many small businesses open accounts on every platform, post for two weeks, then disappear. A smaller, consistent effort beats that cycle every time.

Choose One Platform First

Go where your customers already spend time. For local shops that is often Facebook or WhatsApp; for visual products, Instagram. Master one before adding another.

Post What Helps, Not Only What Sells

Answer common questions, show how products are made, and share customer results with permission. If every post is an advertisement, people stop looking.

A Realistic Schedule

Three good posts a week, every week, outperform daily posts that stop after a month. Prepare a week of posts in one sitting.

Reply Like a Human

Answer comments and messages quickly and politely, even complaints. Public, calm replies build more trust than any advertisement.

Measure One Thing

Each month, check which single post brought the most enquiries — and make more like it. Simple observation beats complicated dashboards at this size.

Gardening in Small Spaces: Grow Fresh Herbs on Your Balcony

October 20, 2026 · By BrightGuide Daily Team

GR

Fresh herbs are expensive in shops and cheap to grow at home. Even a small balcony or a bright window can hold a useful mini garden.

Start With Easy Herbs

Mint, coriander, green chillies and basil forgive beginner mistakes and grow fast. Start with two or three types instead of ten.

Pots and Soil

Any pot works if it has a drainage hole at the bottom. Fill with loose garden soil mixed with a little compost; roots need air as much as water.

Light and Water

Most herbs want at least four to five hours of sunlight. Water when the top layer of soil feels dry — daily deep watering drowns more plants than drought does.

Harvest Little and Often

Pinching the top leaves regularly makes herb plants grow bushier instead of tall and weak. Use what you pinch in that day's cooking.

Your first harvest, even a handful of mint leaves for tea, makes the effort feel worth it — and usually leads to a bigger garden next season.

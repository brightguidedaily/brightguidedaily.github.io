Kids and Screen Time: A Balanced Guide for Parents

October 10, 2026 · By BrightGuide Daily Team

Arguments about phones and tablets are common in almost every home. Clear, agreed rules work better than daily negotiations.

Agree the Rules Together

Children follow rules they helped create. Decide together which hours are screen-free — meals and the hour before bed are a good start.

Quality Matters as Much as Quantity

Creating, learning and video-calling family is very different from endless passive scrolling. Guide children towards active, creative uses.

Offer Real Alternatives

Screens win when nothing else is available. Sports, drawing, board games and helping in the kitchen compete surprisingly well when they are easy to start.

Be the Example

Children copy what they see. If phones are away during family meals for everyone — including parents — the rule feels fair and sticks.

Watch for Warning Signs

Sleep problems, dropping school interest or anger when the device is removed are signals to tighten limits and, if needed, discuss with a teacher or doctor.

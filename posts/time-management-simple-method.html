Time Management: A Simple Method for Busy People

October 16, 2026 · By BrightGuide Daily Team

Time management advice often fails because it is too complicated to maintain. This method has only three parts and needs nothing but paper.

Part 1: The Brain Dump

Every evening, write down everything on your mind: tasks, worries, ideas. An empty mind sleeps better and starts the morning clear.

Part 2: Pick Three

From the list, choose the three tasks that would make tomorrow a good day even if nothing else got done. Circle them. Everything else is optional bonus.

Part 3: First Task Before Phone

Start the first circled task before checking messages or social media. The first hour sets the tone; protect it fiercely.

Review Weekly

Once a week, look at what repeatedly stays unfinished. Either it is not important — delete it — or it is too big — split it into a first small step.

The goal is not a perfect schedule. It is a day where the important things reliably get done and the rest stops causing guilt.

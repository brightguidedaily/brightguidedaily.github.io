Home Workout for Beginners: No Equipment Needed

October 22, 2026 · By BrightGuide Daily Team

HW

Exercise does not require a gym membership. Your own body weight is enough to build strength and fitness, especially as a beginner.

The Simple Routine

  1. Squats — 10 repetitions
  2. Wall push-ups or floor push-ups — 8 repetitions
  3. Glute bridges — 12 repetitions
  4. Bird-dog — 8 per side
  5. Standing march in place — 60 seconds

Rest one minute and repeat the circuit two or three times. The full session takes about twenty minutes.

Warm Up and Cool Down

Spend three minutes marching, swinging your arms and rotating joints before starting. Finish with gentle stretching and slow breathing.

How Often

Three sessions a week on alternate days is a strong start. Muscles grow during rest days, not during the workout itself.

Stay Safe

Move slowly and stop any exercise that causes sharp pain. If you have a medical condition or a long gap since last exercise, consult a doctor before starting a new routine.

Consistency beats intensity. Twenty easy minutes repeated for a month will change how you feel more than one exhausting day.

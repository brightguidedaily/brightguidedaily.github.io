Emergency Fund Basics: Your First and Most Important Saving

October 24, 2026 · By BrightGuide Daily Team

EF

An emergency fund is money kept aside for genuine surprises: a medical bill, a broken phone, or a gap between jobs. It is the foundation every other financial plan stands on.

How Much Do You Need

A common target is three to six months of essential expenses — not your full salary, just rent, food, bills and transport. Beginners can start with a smaller first goal of one month.

Where to Keep It

Keep the fund in a separate, easy-to-access savings account. It should not be invested in anything whose value moves up and down, because emergencies do not wait for good market days.

How to Build It Without Pain

Transfer a fixed small amount on payday, before spending begins. Even a modest weekly transfer grows steadily, and automation removes the need for monthly willpower.

When to Use It — and Refill It

Use it only for real emergencies, not sales or celebrations. After using any part of it, make refilling your first saving priority again.

An emergency fund turns a crisis into an inconvenience. Start with whatever amount you can, but start this month.
